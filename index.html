Wind Valley

a body-flown sky simulator

Spread your arms Wide arms = wings out. The wider you stretch, the more speed you carry.
Tilt to bank Drop one arm and raise the other — the plane rolls that way and carves a turn.
Both arms up / down Raise both arms to climb, lower them to dive toward the sea.
Flap twice to boost Beat your wings up-and-down twice, quickly, and a gust hurls you forward.

Video never leaves this machine — pose tracking runs entirely in your browser.
